Biography    [back top]

I am an Assistant Professor at Southeast University (SEU), Nanjing, China. From 2025 to 2026, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Telecommunications Circuits Laboratory (TCL) of EPFL, and I also received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from EPFL in 2025, under the supervision of Prof. Andreas Burg. I received my M.S. from Southeast University in 2021, under the supervision of Prof. Xiaohu You and Prof. Chuan Zhang, and my B.S. from Nanjing University of Science and Technology in 2018.

I have several successful tape-outs at 16 nm, 28 nm, and 65 nm process nodes. My research interests include:

  • Information theory and practice
  • Algorithms and VLSI implementations for B5G/6G
  • AI x 6G chip design
  • Edge-AI architectures and accelerators
